Connect Ask TRXL to your AI

Ask TRXL lets your own AI assistant search the TRXL archive (episodes, Leadership Edge newsletters and Field Reports) and answer with the episode, date, speaker and a link. Setup takes about two minutes.

Your personal link comes in the email from Evan. It looks like this:
https://dfdfouvctjkhqtlwehtk.supabase.co/functions/v1/mcp?key=YOUR_KEY
Treat it like a password. It's tied to your membership and can be turned off. Wherever you see YOUR_KEY below, use your own.

ClaudeChatGPTCursorGrok

Claude (claude.ai)

  1. Settings, then Connectors, then Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Ask TRXL and paste your link.
  3. When it asks how to connect, choose the no sign-in option.

Claude Code or another MCP app: add the link as a remote HTTP server.

ChatGPT

  1. Click Plugins in the sidebar at the upper left. Then click Add in the upper right and choose Add custom MCP server.
  2. Name it Ask TRXL and paste your link into the Server URL box.
  3. Change Authentication from OAuth to the no-authentication option. Leaving it on OAuth gives "Couldn't discover OAuth settings."
  4. Tick the box that says you understand the risk, then click Create as a plugin.

This worked on a free ChatGPT account. If you don't see Plugins, your ChatGPT may be on an older layout: look under Settings, then Connectors.

Cursor

  1. In Cursor, click Customize in the upper left and select the MCPs pill. Scroll to the bottom and click New MCP server. This opens ~/.cursor/mcp.json. (Older versions: Cursor Settings, then Tools & MCP, then Add new MCP server.)
  2. Add this, replacing YOUR_KEY with the key from your personal link (the part after key=): 
    {
  "mcpServers": {
    "ask-trxl": {
      "url": "https://dfdfouvctjkhqtlwehtk.supabase.co/functions/v1/mcp",
      "headers": { "Authorization": "Bearer YOUR_KEY" }
    }
  }
}
  3. Save. ask-trxl should show a green dot and four tools. If not, toggle it off and on, or restart Cursor.

Red dot and no useful error? The key is almost always missing, mistyped, or still says YOUR_KEY. Cursor doesn't say so. Check the key first.

Don't commit mcp.json to a repo, since it holds your key.

Grok

  1. In the Grok app, click Connect Apps in the lower left, next to your account, and scroll to the bottom of the page. Click Add a custom connector.
    On the web, go to grok.com/connectors instead and click New Connector in the upper right, then Custom.
  2. Name it Ask TRXL and paste your link into Server URL. Your key is already in the link, so there's nothing else to enter.
  3. Click Add Connector.

On a Grok Business or Enterprise plan, a team admin may need to enable connectors first.

Then try it

Start a new chat and ask: "Use Ask TRXL: what can you do, and what's in the library?" Then try:

  • How has talk about AI in architecture firms changed since 2021?
  • Who has said something about firms owning their project data, and what did they say?
  • What did the series partners disagree about on AI wrappers?

Make it use Ask TRXL instead of the web

  • Start with "Use Ask TRXL to..." or "Search the TRXL archive for...". Add "Use only Ask TRXL, no web search" if it wanders.
  • Check the connector is switched on for that chat. A new chat can start with it off.
  • For a standing setup, put this in a Claude Project, a Cursor rule or your custom instructions: "For anything about TRXL episodes, guests or AEC technology, search Ask TRXL first and answer from it. Don't use the web unless I ask."

How to read the answers

Transcript passages are speech-to-text, so your assistant should paraphrase them, not quote them. Field Report and newsletter text is Evan's own writing, and results say so. Feature Spotlight sessions are vendors demoing their own products. Every answer should carry a date, because something said in 2021 may not hold now.

What gets logged

Evan keeps a record of what your assistant asks the library, to see which topics people want and where the library falls short. He doesn't see your conversation, and he looks at results as totals, not by person. Please don't put client or firm details in your questions.
